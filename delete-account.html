Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sports to People account and associated personal data by emailing Sports2ppl@gmail.com.

What to include

Send the email address associated with your account and write “Account Deletion” in the subject.

What happens next?

We will verify the request as necessary to protect the account owner, then process deletion subject to legitimate legal, safety and fraud-prevention retention requirements.

Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or identity documents by email unless we specifically request a secure verification method.
