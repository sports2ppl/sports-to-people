Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This page is a launch-ready plain-language template. Before commercial launch, have it reviewed for the countries where Sports to People operates.

Who operates Sports to People?

Sports to People is operated by Zoran Milacic, Ljubljana, Slovenia, under the brand identity ZocaSport. Contact: Sports2ppl@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process account details, profile information, profile photos, selected sports, approximate location, games and team participation, rankings and ratings, messages, reports and blocks, subscription status, and technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

Location

Location is optional. The product is designed to show approximate distance or area rather than another player's exact location. You can disable location access through your device settings.

Messages and safety

First contact between players is kept inside the app. Users can block and report other users. Safety reports may be reviewed to enforce our Community Guidelines and protect the community.

Children and young people

Sports to People uses additional privacy and interaction protections for users under 18. Real-money competitions are restricted to adults and may remain unavailable in jurisdictions where additional approval is required.

Subscriptions and payments

Where available, subscriptions are processed through the relevant app store or approved payment provider. Payment card details are not intended to be stored directly by Sports to People.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only as needed to provide the service, maintain safety, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. You may request account deletion through Account Deletion.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing. Contact us at Sports2ppl@gmail.com.
