Community Guidelines

Sports to People should feel like a place where people can find a game, meet teammates and enjoy sport safely.

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate or targeted abuse.

Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private information, exact location, phone number or personal contact details without permission.

Play honestly

No fake results, ranking manipulation, cheating, scams or coordinated abuse of competitions.

Keep minors safe

Users under 18 receive additional protections. Do not attempt to move inappropriate conversations with minors outside the platform.

Report and block

Use in-app reporting and blocking. For urgent safety concerns, contact local emergency services. For platform support: Sports2ppl@gmail.com.

Moderation

We may investigate reports and remove content or restrict accounts that violate these guidelines.
